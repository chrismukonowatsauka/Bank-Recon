About

This tool reconciles a bank statement to the ledger. It matches lines on the bank reference, even when the reference is buried in a long ledger description, then on date and amount. It lists what explains the difference: deposits in transit, unpresented payments, bank charges and interest not yet in the ledger, duplicates and amount errors.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or upload leaves your device.

How to use it

  1. Copy the bank statement from Excel, a CSV or online banking and paste it into the first box. You can also drag an Excel or CSV file onto the box, or use Upload file.
  2. Do the same with the ledger for the same account and period.
  3. Enter each opening balance, if the extracts don't start at zero.
  4. Select Reconcile. The Excel working file downloads by itself; Export to Excel downloads it again.
  5. Clear empties both sides to start the next account.

Columns

Date, details, reference, GL account, and amount (or debit and credit) are found automatically. If one is read wrongly, open Columns under the box and change it. Your choice is remembered next time.

The reconciliation statement

It starts from the balance per the bank statement, lists every item that explains the difference under its heading, and ends at the balance per the ledger. The unexplained difference should be zero.

How lines are matched

  • On the bank reference, wherever it appears in the ledger description
  • On date and amount, within three days
  • Ledger items in the last five days of the period are treated as timing differences
  • Pairs with the same amount but nothing else in common are flagged "Check this match"

The Excel export

Three sheets: the reconciliation statement, every ledger line and every bank line, each with its status, what it was matched to, and any difference. Confirm the "Check this match" items in the file.
