How to use

  1. Paste or open your bank statement on the left and your cash book on the right. Click a grid and press Ctrl+V. A header row is optional, and extra columns are fine.
  2. Check the column picks above each grid. Change any with its dropdown and the app remembers your choice for files laid out the same way.
  3. Enter the opening balances if your files cover the period only. The balances then run through to the reconciliation statement.
  4. Deal with "Check these" first: confirm or reject each suggested pair. Then work through the lines under "To look at".
  5. Export to Excel for the reconciliation statement, the cash book and the bank statement, each line marked Matched or Unmatched.

How lines are matched

First on the bank reference, wherever it appears in the cash book description, even when the clerk pasted the whole bank line. Then on date and amount, using the narration to choose between lines that share both. Then the same amount a few days apart: automatic when the narration agrees, otherwise it goes to "Check these".

Settings

The outstanding window decides which cash book items near period end count as timing differences. The date tolerance is how far apart a matched pair may be. Suggestions look a little further, up to the number of days you set.
